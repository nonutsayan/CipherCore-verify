Platform architecture

Four engines. One evidence trail.

Three engines gather evidence. The fourth makes sure a person decides.

Regulatory verification workflow

From dossier to decision, with the evidence attached.

Step 1 of 4

Dossier ingestion

The application and its six mandatory PDFs enter a secure pipeline. Each file gets a SHA-256 fingerprint on arrival.

Step 2 of 4

Automated triangulation

OCR extracts the fields. Authority adapters check state registries for live status, so every claim is compared with its source.

Step 3 of 4

Anomaly flagging

Discrepancies in dates, acreage, workstation counts or duplicate PANs are highlighted with severity tags, ready for review.

Step 4 of 4

Official sign-off

The officer examines the evidence, records the decision and logs a cryptographic justification that cannot be edited quietly later.

Statutory safeguard

AI flags are advisory. A person signs.

No auto-rejections without human review.

  • Flags are preliminaryThey support a decision. They are not proof of forgery or non-compliance.
  • Officers decideApprovals, show-cause notices and deficiency orders need an authorised officer's digital signature.
  • Everything is loggedEach action lands in an audit ledger built on SHA-256 hashes.

CipherCore Verify

A prototype built in response to a Smart India Hackathon problem statement. Automated flags are preliminary decision-support outputs. Final approvals, deficiency notices and inspection orders remain with competent human authorities. This is not an official AICTE system.
