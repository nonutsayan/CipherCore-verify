Automated Verification Engines

Four distinct validation engines operating in parallel to evaluate institutional legitimacy.

1. OCR & Forensic Tamper Check

Extracts machine-readable text layers, compares font encodings, and identifies post-issuance raster overlays. Cross-compares document dates, institutional names, and land acreage across all uploaded PDFs to catch doctored scans.

SHA-256 Fingerprinting Duplicate Hash Cache QR Payload Verification

2. Issuing Authority Gateway

Direct API validation against authoritative government registries. Queries State Fire Services portals for active NOC certificates, DigiLocker State Land Registries for CTS property cards, and University Affiliation records.

State Fire Services API DigiLocker Land Extract Class-3 DSC Verification

3. APH Infrastructure Audit

Evaluates institutional capacity against AICTE Approval Process Handbook (APH 2024-27) norms. Validates contiguous land area (Rural vs Metro), computer terminal density (1:1 per intake), and barrier-free ramp standards.

FSR 1:20 Compliance Geo-Coordinates Validation Lab Carpet Area Norms

4. Statutory Human Decision Gate

Automated AI flags serve strictly as advisory decision-support. Official approval, show-cause notices, or deficiency orders must be reviewed and digitally signed by an authorized scrutiny officer with full audit logging.

Statutory Requirement

6 Mandatory Documents for Extension of Approval (EoA)

AICTE APH Section 2.1
1. Fire Safety Certificate (NOC)
Issued by Competent Fire Authority. Valid through relevant academic session.
2. Land Ownership Deed
Registered sale deed or government land lease. Minimum contiguous land area.
3. University Affiliation Letter
Issued by affiliating state/central university for approved course branches.
4. Faculty Roster & PAN List
Authenticated roster with unique PANs meeting 1:20 faculty-to-student ratio.
5. Approved Building Plan
Sanctioned by Municipal Corporation / Town Planning Authority with built-up area.
6. Fee Payment Receipt
Proof of statutory regulatory fee payment deposited into the AICTE treasury.

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026 | Domain: verify.ciphercore.in

1. Purpose of Data Processing

CipherCore Verify processes institutional compliance applications and supporting documents strictly for application validation, document authentication, and regulatory scrutiny. Institutional and faculty data is never sold or shared with unauthorized third parties.

2. Categories of Records Processed

The portal processes institutional application forms, uploaded regulatory PDFs (Fire Safety NOCs, Land Registry Deeds, University Affiliation Letters, Faculty Rosters, Building Plans, and Fee Receipts), SHA-256 cryptographic file hashes, and official review logs.

3. Access Control & Security Logging

Access to institutional records and issuing-authority verification gateways is role-based and restricted to authorized review officers. Every document check and official decision is logged with a timestamp and actor identifier in the Audit Log.

4. Contact

For privacy or data governance inquiries, contact the CipherCore Data Protection Desk at privacy@ciphercore.in.

Terms and Conditions

Last Updated: October 2026 | Domain: verify.ciphercore.in

1. Authorized Portal Usage

CipherCore Verify is restricted to authorized compliance reviewers and institutional representatives. Users must only upload genuine institutional records or authorized test datasets.

2. Decision-Support Safeguard

All automated OCR comparisons, metadata tampering flags, and infrastructure checks are preliminary decision-support outputs only. Automated flags require human verification and do not constitute final proof of forgery or non-compliance.

3. Mandatory Human Review Gate

Final regulatory approval, deficiency notices, or physical inspection orders remain solely with competent human authorities and must be logged through the Record Official Decision workflow.
